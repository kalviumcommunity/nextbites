Margherita Pizza

Blistered crust, San Marzano sauce, melty mozzarella, fresh basil.

⏱ 35 minMediumItalian

Ingredients

  • 1 pizza dough ball
  • 1/2 cup tomato sauce
  • 125g fresh mozzarella
  • Fresh basil leaves
  • Olive oil, salt

Method

  1. Heat the oven as hot as it goes with a tray inside.
  2. Stretch the dough thin and spread the sauce.
  3. Tear mozzarella over the top and drizzle oil.
  4. Bake 7 to 9 minutes until the crust is charred.
  5. Finish with basil and a pinch of salt.
