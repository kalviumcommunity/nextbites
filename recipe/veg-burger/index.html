Veg Burger

Crispy spiced patty, soft bun, tangy sauce, crunchy veg.

⏱ 30 minEasyAmerican

Ingredients

  • 2 burger buns
  • 1 cup mashed potato and peas
  • Breadcrumbs, spices
  • Lettuce, tomato, onion
  • Mayo and ketchup

Method

  1. Mix potato, peas, spices and shape patties.
  2. Coat in breadcrumbs and pan-fry until golden.
  3. Toast the buns.
  4. Layer sauce, patty and veg.
  5. Close and serve warm.
